## Brand & Style

The design system bridges authentic South Indian culinary tradition and celebration culture with contemporary fine-dining hospitality. It embodies sacred warmth, generous hospitality, and quiet architectural luxury. 

### Design Movement
**Heritage Modernism**: A balance of classical architectural restraint and editorial warmth. It blends tactile cream surfaces, deep temple-forest greens, calibrated gold hairline borders, and generous editorial margins to convey timelessness without looking antiquated.

### Tone & Character
- **Sanctified & Welcoming:** Evokes brass lamps (*diyas*), fresh plantain leaves, and aromatic filter coffee.
- **Architectural & Dignified:** Inspired by traditional Chettinad pillars, courtyards, and Brindavana hall grandeur, streamlined into clean digital interfaces.
- **Warm & Crisp:** Avoids high-tech coldness or flat minimalism by utilizing rich earth-rooted undertones and refined serif typography.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in temple gardens, seasoned teak wood, warm brassware, and auspicious celebration tones:

- **Primary (`#153E28` | Forest Deep):** Represents plantain leaves, auspicious tulsi foliage, and dignified calm. Used for high-emphasis containers, active navigation states, primary buttons, and hero banners. A deeper variant (`#0F2C1D`) serves for elevated contrast and night overlays.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27` | Burnished Brass / Gold):** Represents traditional brass serviceware and auspicious festivity. Used deliberately for hairline dividers, badge outlines, icon accents, and tertiary CTAs. An alternate lighter tint (`#D4AF37`) is reserved for hover highlights and delicate decorative filigree.
- **Tertiary (`#6B1D2F` | Muted Kumkum Maroon):** Represents wedding celebration and sacred warmth. Used for culinary badges (e.g., "Chef's Special", "Brindavana Hall Booking Available"), promotional ribboning, and festive markers.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FAF7F2` | Ivory Sandhanam): Replaces harsh white with organic parchment warmth.
  - Layered Surface (`#F4EFE6` | Steamed Payasam Cream): Used for inset panels, elevated cards, and menu groupings.
  - Deep Teak Neutral (`#3E2723`): Used for secondary structural lines, heavy footer backdrops, and rich thematic blocks.
- **Text & Reading Layers:**
  - High Emphasis (`#1E293B` | Dark Slate Ink): Crisp, highly legible reading text with a softened edge against warm ivory.
  - Muted Copy (`#475569` | Tempered Slate): Secondary descriptions, dietary notices, and banquet specifications.

## Typography

The type pairing conveys dignified heritage through literary serif titles grounded by an open, highly scannable sans-serif foundation for practical booking flows and menu discovery:

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Set in `Playfair Display`. Its graceful stroke contrast echoes South Indian brass and architectural carvings. Always applied to hero statements, banquet room names, and culinary section titles.
- **Body & Operational Copy:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Brings clean clarity to ingredient lists, price points, dietary specifications, and reservation schedules without visual friction.
- **Labels & Micro-Accents:** Small eyebrow headers and section tags (e.g., "PURE VEGETARIAN DELICACIES", "HALL CAPACITY") should be set in uppercase with tracking (`0.08em`) to impart an organized hospitality feel.

## Layout & Spacing

A balanced 12-column layout on desktop converts to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. Margins are generous to echo the breathing space of traditional courtyard architecture.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (4-column grid, 1.25rem outer margins, compact card stacks)
  - Tablet: `640px - 1024px` (6-column grid, 2rem outer margins)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12-column grid, max-width 1280px centered, 3rem outer margins)
- **Rhythm Rules:**
  - Vertical section blocks alternate between natural `#FAF7F2` canvas and framed `#F4EFE6` bands with `3rem` to `5rem` vertical spacing.
  - Culinary dish lists prioritize relaxed vertical cadence (`space-md` gaps) over tight utility packing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, low-contrast shadows combined with structural gold perimeter lines instead of heavy synthetic drop-shadows:

- **Ambient Shadows:** Cast with a warm teak/amber undertone rather than pure grey. Base shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(62, 39, 35, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(62, 39, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Card Framing:** Elevated layers rest on `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFE6` surfaces delineated with hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.25)`).
- **Floating Modals & Datepickers:** Raised elevation uses `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(21, 62, 40, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(62, 39, 35, 0.08)`.
- **Hero & Banner Depth:** Deep forest green overlays on photography use a linear bottom-to-top gradient starting at `rgba(15, 44, 29, 0.88)` terminating at `rgba(15, 44, 29, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, gentle curvature (`roundedness: 2`, where standard components utilize `0.5rem` / `8px` corner radii) to evoke hospitality and comfort:

- **Cards & Image Containers:** Feature soft `0.75rem` (`rounded-lg`) to `1rem` outer corners to soften food photography and architectural images.
- **Buttons & Chips:** Feature consistent `0.5rem` curvature. Buttons avoid hard clinical corners or hyper-casual full pills.
- **Ornamental Frames:** Banquet feature highlights can utilize an arched top border (`rounded-t-[3rem]`) to subtly echo traditional Indian Mandap and doorway silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Book Hall", "Reserve Table"):** Solid Forest Green (`#153E28`) background, crisp white typography, subtle gold hover border (`1px solid #C59B27`). Focus ring uses a warm gold tint.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "View Menu", "Explore Spaces"):** Outlined in Forest Green (`1.5px solid #153E28`) or Burnished Gold (`1.5px solid #C59B27`) with Slate text and an Ivory background.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Playfair Display italic or Plus Jakarta Sans with a persistent hairline gold underline offset by `4px`.

### Cards & Menu Items
- **Restaurant Menu Cards:** Contained within `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFE6` blocks with a delicate `1px solid rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.2)` border. Each item features the dish name in Playfair Display, price point right-aligned in Dark Slate, and dietary icons (green pure-veg square/circle emblem).
- **Party Hall Showcase Cards:** Full-width image header with arched corner styling, occupancy badge in Kumkum Maroon (`#6B1D2F`), and primary CTA pinned to bottom right.

### Badges & Chips
- **Pure Vegetarian Trust Stamp:** Crisp border in Primary Green (`#153E28`) enclosing the canonical green circle emblem with typography in `label-sm`.
- **Hall Amenity Chips (AC, Seating, Catering):** Muted Ivory-cream container (`#F4EFE6`) bordered with gold hairline (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.3)`), dark slate label text.

### Form Inputs & Datepickers
- **Input Fields:** Warm ivory surface (`#FFFFFF` with `#FAF7F2` container inset), `1.5px` border in `#E2D9CC`. On focus, transitions cleanly to `1.5px solid #153E28` with an ambient glow of `rgba(21, 62, 40, 0.1)`.
- **Date & Guest Selectors:** Calendar active days highlighted with Forest Green circular fills; wedding auspicious dates highlighted with small Gold indicators.

### Decorative Dividers
- Hairline rules styled as a single centered brass motif or 3-dot cluster (`#C59B27`) separating major editorial sections.
